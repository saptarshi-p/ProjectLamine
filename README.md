# ⚽ ProjectLamine
### *Before the big clubs. Before the headlines. ProjectLamine finds football's next stars using ML.*

---

## 🌟 Overview
ProjectLamine is a data science project that identifies underrated football 
players before they become household names — inspired by how Lamine Yamal 
went from unknown to world-class.

Using FIFA 23 data, I built a Hidden Gem Scoring system that surfaces young, 
high-potential players flying under the radar in lower leagues.

---

## 🔍 How It Works

Players with a high gap between potential and current ability, 
combined with young age, are flagged as hidden gems.

---

## ✅ Real World Validation

|---|---|---|---|---|
| G. Kuol | 62 | 82 | Top 10 | Signed by Newcastle United ✅ |
| C. Riquelme | 60 | 83 | #1 Pick | Signed by Colo-Colo ✅ |

---

## 📊 Key Findings
- 🇦🇷 **Argentina's Liga Profesional** produces the most hidden gems
- 🎂 **Age 21-23** is the sweet spot for undiscovered talent
- 📈 Potential-Overall gap of 8+ points = strong hidden gem signal
